# PZ Survival V1

Taste-gate repair after V0 feedback.

## Objective
Make the survival slice visibly read as a real neighborhood with houses/interiors and make inventory/looting usable.

## Non-goals
- no new blockchain work
- no new combat spectacle layer
- no Project Zomboid proprietary assets/source/maps/UI copying
- no merge authority

## Acceptance
- buildings read as houses/store at first glance from the browser capture
- roofs visibly cover buildings outside and cut away/fade when the survivor enters
- doors/windows are visually legible parts of buildings
- interiors contain recognizable authored furniture/decor cues
- inventory can open/close independently
- interacting with a container opens a functional two-pane transfer UI
- TAKE / TAKE ALL / consume / bandage buttons work
- save/load/new remain functional
- deterministic simulation and escrow regression gates stay green
