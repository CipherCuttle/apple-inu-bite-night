# Rescue + City Level V0

Routine powerup drops are removed. Combo tiers remain for score, while normal cut-speed bonuses are reduced to 1.04x / 1.08x / 1.12x.

Once per run, when HP falls to exactly 1, Last Chance fires: enemies/world advance one of every four fixed ticks for 150 ticks (~2.5s), player attacks remain full-rate, the player gets 60 invulnerable ticks, and one nearby LAST BITE pickup appears. LAST BITE heals 2 HP and grants 1.25x cut speed for 120 ticks.

SPACE, E, Q and Shift-release now use fixed-tick buffers matching the responsive mouse input path. Restart clears all buffers.

Kenney Pico-8 City CC0 sample map is imported from pinned Tiddybub/2d-assets revision e0cbe0d995554a490d4c182fe9beb8769ffbb606. Its 55x30 8px layout is rendered at 2x under authoritative building collision rectangles, creating streets, alleys and chokepoints.
